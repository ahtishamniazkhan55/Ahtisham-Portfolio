Redesigning Roze Remit: Making Global Money Transfers Simple, Fast, and Trustworthy

Website redesign case study

Roze Remit helps people move money across borders, so trust, clarity, and speed can determine whether someone completes a transfer or walks away. The website needed more than a visual refresh, it needed a clearer experience that communicated its value quickly, eased hesitation, and guided users naturally from discovery to action.

The redesign focused on creating a modern, mobile-first experience built around the questions users care about most: Where can I send money? What rate will I get? Is my money secure? How does it work? And what should I do next?

I restructured the experience across the Homepage, Country-specific transfer corridors, Rates and limits, Referral journeys, FAQs, App promotion, and Login and Signup flows. The result was clearer navigation, a stronger content hierarchy, more purposeful CTAs, and more effective trust-building communication.
